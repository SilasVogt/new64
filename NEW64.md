# new64

A native Linux build of Super Mario 64 based on
[sm64coopdx](https://github.com/coop-deluxe/sm64coopdx) v1.5.1, with two
new courses: **Course 16, Sky Garden Isles** and **Course 17, Frostfire
Fjord**, plus a new enemy (Lundi) and a new geyser object.

## Build and play

You need your own US ROM (`Super Mario 64 (USA).z64`, SHA1
`9bef1128717f958171a4afac3ed78ee2bb4e86ce`). It is gitignored and must never
be committed.

```sh
cp ~/Roms/N64/"Super Mario 64 (USA).z64" baserom.us.z64
make -j$(nproc)
./play.sh
```

`play.sh` hosts a single-player game that only listens on localhost and
keeps its saves in `~/.local/share/new64`. The save layout differs from
upstream coopdx (see below), so do not point it at an upstream save folder.

Build dependencies on CachyOS/Arch: `sdl2 glew curl zlib python`.

## Sky Garden Isles

Floating islands above the clouds. Enter through the green warp pipe on the
castle grounds, just left of where Mario starts. Collecting a star, dying or
leaving the course brings you back out of that pipe.

| # | Star | How |
|---|------|-----|
| 1 | King Bob-omb's Sky Arena | Climb the stone steps east of the garden and throw King Bob-omb three times (act 1). |
| 2 | Top of the Cloud Tower | Climb the spiral steps around the tower in the middle. |
| 3 | Red Coins on the Isles | Eight red coins along the islets to the west; the star appears on the last islet. |
| 4 | Blast to the Lonely Isle | Talk to the pink Bob-omb buddy, then fire the cannon on the stone pedestal at the island to the northeast. A high lob (about 73 degrees) lands it; the tree there can be grabbed. |
| 5 | Five Secrets in the Sky | Five hidden spots: behind the tower, behind the spawn point, on the tall north pillar, at the far east edge of the arena, and on a step halfway up the tower. |
| 6 | The Box Above the Bridge | The ! box over the platform on the north bridge; double jump into it. |
| 100 | 100 coins | 133 coins in the course (red coins count 2). |

## Frostfire Fjord (course 17)

Fire and ice, Iceland style, across three connected areas. Enter through the
second green warp pipe on the castle grounds, just south of the first.

- **Highlands:** black sand beach, basalt sea stacks with stepped columns,
  moss lava fields, a geyser field with a basalt spire, a milky blue hot
  spring, a turf-house village and church, a glacier with blue ice cliffs and
  a waterfall, and a volcano with a lava river and a crater lake. It snows.
  The lava, the waterfall and the sea flow (scrolling textures).
- **Glacier ice cave** (walk into the dark mouth in the ice cliff): a crystal
  hall over a frozen lake, a maze of side tunnels, an upper gallery and an
  ice slide back down.
- **Magma hall** (drop into the skylight pit on the volcano's west flank): a
  lava tube with fire bars and flamethrowers that opens onto a lava lake,
  with an ice floe in the middle.

| # | Star | How |
|---|------|-----|
| 1 | The Big Chill in the Magma Hall | Push the Big Chill Bully off the ice floe into the lava (act 1). Cross on the basalt stepping stones. |
| 2 | Geyser Ride to the Basalt Spire | Stand on the geyser by the spire; when it erupts, drift onto the ledge column, then ride its geyser onto the spire. |
| 3 | Red Coins in the Glacier Cave | Eight red coins in the ice cave; the star appears over the pillar in the crystal hall. |
| 4 | Lundi Patrol on the Sea Stacks | Defeat all five Lundi puffins; the star appears above the tallest sea stack. Climb the basalt columns from the beach. |
| 5 | Behind the Roaring Waterfall | Walk through the waterfall into the alcove behind it. |
| 6 | Stepping Stones in the Crater | Climb the volcano (every side is walkable), go round the rim to where the lava river starts, step down onto the basalt column there and cross the stepping stones; hop up onto the spire in the lava lake. |
| 100 | 100 coins | About 240 coins across the three areas (coins carry over between areas). |

### Lundi, the frostfire puffin (new enemy)

Lundi (Icelandic for puffin) perch on the sea stacks. When Mario comes near
they take off, circle their nest and dive-bomb him. Stomp or punch one to
defeat it (2 coins). Contact from the side hurts. Code:
`src/game/behaviors/new64_frostfire.inc.c`; the model is generated by
`tools/new64/gen_ffj.py`.

### Geysers (new object)

Geysers erupt on a cycle (bubbling, then a tall steam column). Standing on the
vent during an eruption throws Mario into a slow twirl he can steer. The
launch speed is the geyser's second behavior parameter byte; the hot spring's
geysers are decorative (speed 0).

## What changed from upstream

- `levels/course_defines.h`: `COURSE_SGI` and `COURSE_FFJ` are main courses
  16 and 17; bonus courses moved to 18-27. Lua constants, docs and the display list registry were
  regenerated with the upstream autogen scripts.
- `levels/level_defines.h`: `LEVEL_SGI` and `LEVEL_FFJ` replace the unused
  level slots 35 and 37.
- `include/behavior_table.h`, `data/behavior_data.c`: `bhvNew64Lundi` and
  `bhvNew64Geyser`.
- `src/game/behaviors/bully.inc.c`: the Big Chill Bully's star position and
  fall height in Frostfire Fjord.
- `src/game/save_file.*`: the never-used cap position is no longer saved,
  which makes room for the new course's star and coin bytes and keeps the
  512-byte EEPROM image. Coin score ages for course 17 use two spare menu
  bytes. **Saves are not compatible with upstream coopdx.**
- `text/us/new64_courses.inc.c`: course and act names (vanilla text is read
  from the ROM; ours is generated by `tools/new64/gen_text.py`).
- `levels/castle_grounds/script.c`: the two warp pipes.
- `src/game/behaviors/king_bobomb.inc.c`: King Bob-omb's star position in
  this course.
- `src/menu/file_select.c`: course 16 on the score screen.
- `src/pc/update_checker.c`: no upstream update prompts (they would replace
  this build).
- `src/pc/network/socket/socket.c`: `NEW64_BIND_LOCALHOST` binds the host to
  loopback (used by `play.sh` and the tests).
- `src/pc/gfx/gfx_sdl.c`: `NEW64_SHOT_DIR` dumps frames for automated
  screenshots.

## Editing the courses

The levels are generated. Edit the layout, then regenerate and build:

```sh
python3 tools/new64/gen_level.py . /tmp/map.png   # Sky Garden Isles
python3 tools/new64/gen_ffj.py . /tmp/ffj.png     # Frostfire Fjord (shared code in levelgen.py)
python3 tools/new64/gen_text.py .                 # course and act names
make -j8
```

`script.c`, `geo.c` and `header.h` in each level folder are hand-written;
everything under `areas/`, the model folders and `layout.h` is generated
(`levels/sgi/areas/1/geo.inc.c` is the one hand-written file there). If
you add display lists or behaviors, rerun `python3 autogen/extract_display_lists.py`
and `python3 autogen/convert_constants.py`.

`levelgen.py` builds terrain from a grid of 320-unit cells. A cell can carry a
region function (material, and a drop or flat level for lava, lakes and
islands); cells are then split along the region boundaries (marching squares),
so lava banks, shores and material patches follow smooth curves instead of
cell-sized steps, with banks walled where the floor drops. Materials can
scroll: the generator writes `levels/ffj/scroll.inc.c`, and
`geo_ffj_scroll_textures` in `levels/ffj/geo.c` moves those textures every
frame.

## Tests

The Lua test mods in `tools/new64/testmods/` drive the game through
`tools/new64/testrun.sh`, which runs a muted, localhost-only host and dumps
frames:

```sh
cp tools/new64/testmods/sgi-flow.lua /tmp/new64-save/mods/new64-test.lua
# coop_stay_in_level_after_star 1 in /tmp/new64-save/sm64config.txt
tools/new64/testrun.sh 95 /tmp/new64-shots/flow
grep new64-flow /tmp/new64-shots/flow/game.log   # expect "FINAL flags 0x7F"
```

- `sgi-flow.lua`: collects all seven stars and checks the save flags.
- `sgi-cannon.lua`: fires the real cannon over a range of pitches.
- `sgi-exit.lua`: pipe entry, star exit back to the pipe, save.
- `ffj-flow.lua`: all 7 Frostfire stars, real geyser rides and Lundi stomps,
  every area warp (expect `FINAL flags 0x7F`).
- `ffj-tour.lua`: camera tour of all three Frostfire areas for screenshots.
- `ffj-walk.lua`: real stick input: climbs the volcano from nine directions
  and walks the crater stones to the spire (expect `REACHED` for every run).
- `shots.lua` via `tools/new64/shots.sh <ffj|sgi> <outdir>`: fixed-camera
  screenshots of both courses and a contact sheet. Compare two runs with
  `python3 tools/new64/shot_sheet.py --compare <before> <after> <out prefix>`.

Delete `sm64_save_file.bin` in the save folder before a flow test, otherwise
stars from the last run are already set.
