# new64

A native Linux build of Super Mario 64 based on
[sm64coopdx](https://github.com/coop-deluxe/sm64coopdx) v1.5.1, with one
new course: **Course 16, Sky Garden Isles**.

## Build and play

You need your own US ROM (`Super Mario 64 (USA).z64`, SHA1
`9bef1128717f958171a4afac3ed78ee2bb4e86ce`). It is gitignored and must never
be committed.

```sh
cp ~/Roms/N64/"Super Mario 64 (USA).z64" baserom.us.z64
make -j$(nproc)
./play.sh
```

`play.sh` hosts a single-player game that only listens on localhost and
keeps its saves in `~/.local/share/new64`. The save layout differs from
upstream coopdx (see below), so do not point it at an upstream save folder.

Build dependencies on CachyOS/Arch: `sdl2 glew curl zlib python`.

## Sky Garden Isles

Floating islands above the clouds. Enter through the green warp pipe on the
castle grounds, just left of where Mario starts. Collecting a star, dying or
leaving the course brings you back out of that pipe.

| # | Star | How |
|---|------|-----|
| 1 | King Bob-omb's Sky Arena | Climb the stone steps east of the garden and throw King Bob-omb three times (act 1). |
| 2 | Top of the Cloud Tower | Climb the spiral steps around the tower in the middle. |
| 3 | Red Coins on the Isles | Eight red coins along the islets to the west; the star appears on the last islet. |
| 4 | Blast to the Lonely Isle | Talk to the pink Bob-omb buddy, then fire the cannon on the stone pedestal at the island to the northeast. A high lob (about 73 degrees) lands it; the tree there can be grabbed. |
| 5 | Five Secrets in the Sky | Five hidden spots: behind the tower, behind the spawn point, on the tall north pillar, at the far east edge of the arena, and on a step halfway up the tower. |
| 6 | The Box Above the Bridge | The ! box over the platform on the north bridge; double jump into it. |
| 100 | 100 coins | 133 coins in the course (red coins count 2). |

## What changed from upstream

- `levels/course_defines.h`: `COURSE_SGI` is main course 16; bonus courses
  moved to 17-26. Lua constants, docs and the display list registry were
  regenerated with the upstream autogen scripts.
- `levels/level_defines.h`: `LEVEL_SGI` replaces the unused level slot 35.
- `src/game/save_file.*`: the never-used cap position is no longer saved,
  which makes room for the new course's star and coin bytes and keeps the
  512-byte EEPROM image. **Saves are not compatible with upstream coopdx.**
- `text/us/new64_courses.inc.c`: course and act names (vanilla text is read
  from the ROM; ours is generated by `tools/new64/gen_text.py`).
- `levels/castle_grounds/script.c`: the warp pipe.
- `src/game/behaviors/king_bobomb.inc.c`: King Bob-omb's star position in
  this course.
- `src/menu/file_select.c`: course 16 on the score screen.
- `src/pc/update_checker.c`: no upstream update prompts (they would replace
  this build).
- `src/pc/network/socket/socket.c`: `NEW64_BIND_LOCALHOST` binds the host to
  loopback (used by `play.sh` and the tests).
- `src/pc/gfx/gfx_sdl.c`: `NEW64_SHOT_DIR` dumps frames for automated
  screenshots.

## Editing the course

The level is generated. Edit the layout in `tools/new64/gen_level.py`, then:

```sh
python3 tools/new64/gen_level.py . /tmp/map.png   # also writes a top-down map
make -j$(nproc)
```

`levels/sgi/script.c`, `geo.c` and `header.h` are hand-written; everything
under `levels/sgi/areas/1/` and `levels/sgi/layout.h` is generated.

## Tests

The Lua test mods in `tools/new64/testmods/` drive the game through
`tools/new64/testrun.sh`, which runs a muted, localhost-only host and dumps
frames:

```sh
cp tools/new64/testmods/sgi-flow.lua /tmp/new64-save/mods/new64-test.lua
# coop_stay_in_level_after_star 1 in /tmp/new64-save/sm64config.txt
tools/new64/testrun.sh 95 /tmp/new64-shots/flow
grep new64-flow /tmp/new64-shots/flow/game.log   # expect "FINAL flags 0x7F"
```

- `sgi-flow.lua`: collects all seven stars and checks the save flags.
- `sgi-cannon.lua`: fires the real cannon over a range of pitches.
- `sgi-exit.lua`: pipe entry, star exit back to the pipe, save.
